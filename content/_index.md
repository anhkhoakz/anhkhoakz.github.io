Anh Khoa writes about privacy, security, and software engineering.
Topics include privacy engineering, secure software development, application security,
Linux, macOS, open-source tools, and developer workflows.

The goal is practical, evidence-aware guidance.
Every article keeps a minimal, performance-first, and privacy-by-design approach.
No hype. No tool collecting. Just trade-offs explained clearly.

What you will find here:

- **Privacy** — digital privacy checklists, browser hardening, email alternatives, and data protection habits.
- **Security** — threat-aware setups, account protection, and defensive security for everyday use.
- **Software engineering** — version control with Git, development workflows, and practical developer tools.

This site is for engineers, privacy advocates, and anyone building or using software
with a security-conscious mindset.
Articles focus on real-world trade-offs, not theoretical perfection.
