## KIRI TH28

This model prioritizes strong cooling output, a clear display,
and a sturdy clamp mount.

{{< figure src="https://ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/S62df07fa31d94699b1e427ddcc7c3157z.png_960x960.png_.avif" alt="TH28 Image" width="960" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async" >}}

### Highlights

Accessories include a durable braided USB-C cable and a sturdy clamp mount.

Real-world power draw is around **35–39 W**,
allowing **rapid cooling** and even surface icing under room conditions.

The display shows power consumption and temperature directly.

The top cover can be removed for cleaning.

The design includes airflow shielding
and channels hot exhaust air toward both sides.

### Points to Consider

The fan is fairly **loud** when operating at high power.
Magnetic holding strength has been improved compared with the V2 version.
The metal contact surface may **scratch the back** of a phone
if used carelessly.
Power consumption is high, so a reliable charger is required.
A charger rated at **40 W or higher** is recommended,
and low-capacity power banks should not be relied upon.

### Usage Recommendations

Use a charger rated at **45 W or higher**,
such as the CUKTECH 65 W or another model that supports USB **Power Delivery**.
Apply PPF film or use the included metal plate to reduce the risk of scratching
the phone's back panel.

## KIRI TH12

This model balances strong cooling with a flexible mount
and AI-assisted control.

{{< figure src="https://ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/Sa8bba157bb9041dfb946b855aac7f0d7q.jpg_960x960q75.jpg_.avif" alt="TH12 Image" width="960" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async" >}}

### 1. KIRI TH12 Overview and Accessories

Included accessories:

- A thick USB-C to USB-C cable.
- A metal plate, although it is slightly small.
- A high-quality clamp with a **1/4-inch thread** for attaching lights, stands,
  or livestreaming accessories.

**Minor drawback:** The clamp is somewhat long
and may occasionally interfere with the screen while gaming.

### 2. KIRI TH12 Design and Build Quality

- Good overall build quality.
- A thermally conductive silicone contact surface.
- Airflow shielding prevents hot exhaust air from blowing directly onto the
  user's hands.
- Magnetic holding strength is among the strongest in its price range.

### 3. KIRI TH12 Real-World Performance

- Strong cooling capability with fast and deep temperature reduction.
- In testing at around **20 W**,
  the unit maintained good temperatures even under demanding conditions.

### 4. KIRI TH12 Power Supply and Durability

Durability depends heavily on power-source quality and usage habits.
A good-quality charger rated between **45 W and 100 W** is recommended.

> **Warning:** Do not place the unit fan-side down on a table
> while it is operating.
> Blocking airflow can overheat components, cause damage,
> or create a fire hazard.

### 5. KIRI TH12 AI Features and Controls

- The LED-display version shows actual power draw in watts.
- Supports **L1, L2, L3**, and **AI** modes.
- AI mode automatically adjusts power according to temperature.
- Includes a physical button for turning off the LED.
- Can remember the previously selected mode after power is disconnected.

### Tips for Longer Service Life

- Avoid impacts and drops.
- Do not block the air intake.
  This is critical because the hot side of the Peltier module requires
  continuous heat dissipation.
- Prevent condensation from flowing into the circuit board, power port, or fan.

### Choosing a Power Source

- **CUKTECH CP3:** A reasonably priced option with a power display,
  suitable for powering the TH12 by itself.
- **MOVESPEED 140 W:** Suitable when sharing one charger with a laptop
  or multiple devices.
- **65 W chargers from AUKEY, URIN, or CUKTECH:** These can work well
  when paired with a high-quality USB-C cable.
- **Power bank:** A 20,000 mAh CUKTECH model is recommended,
  using its USB-C port.
  Avoid USB-A when high power output is required.

Use a good-quality cable.
A cable with an integrated power display makes it easier to monitor the power
delivered to the cooler.

## PIVA X30

This is a strong budget pick focused on raw cooling and simple operation.

{{< figure src="https://ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/Sd7ce17f4e848439a92d0acbd1c415d26e.jpg_960x960q75.jpg_.avif" alt="X30 Image" width="960" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async" >}}

### 1. PIVA X30 Design and Accessories

- Includes a metal plate, user manual, and USB-C to USB-C cable.
- Does not include a clamp; the unit supports magnetic attachment only.
- The body is fairly thick, measuring slightly over **3 cm**.
- Large metal contact surface with a diameter of approximately **5.5 cm**.
- Strong magnetic holding force.
- Large LED assembly.
- Reverse-airflow design helps prevent hot exhaust air from blowing onto the
  user's hands.

### 2. PIVA X30 Power and Features

- Real-world power draw is approximately **27 W**.
- Operates stably with the CUKTECH CP3.
- Four lighting modes correspond to different cooling levels: red, green, blue,
  and purple.
- No temperature display; users must rely on the LED color and real-world feel.

### 3. PIVA X30 Performance and AI

- In a room at approximately **28°C**,
  the unit can reach around **-11°C** and form ice on its surface.
- Blue mode provides automatic temperature control,
  helping reduce condensation and water buildup.
- Suitable for unattended gaming sessions or long-duration use.

### 4. PIVA X30 Summary

The short version is below.

#### PIVA X30 Advantages

- Strong cooling performance.
- Effective airflow design.
- AI mode provides good temperature control.
- Good build quality.

#### PIVA X30 Disadvantages

- No clamp included.
- No direct information display.

**Conclusion:** The PIVA X30 is a strong option in the under-400,000 VND price
range.

## PIVA B3

This version adds a clamp and keeps roughly the same performance profile.

{{< figure src="https://ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/S4056e61bdc49400dad4ababc8fb472b48.jpg_960x960q75.jpg_.avif" alt="B3 Image" width="960" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async" >}}

- Comes with a complete accessory package and includes a clamp,
  unlike the PIVA X30.
- Sturdy body construction.
- Large contact surface with a diameter of approximately **5.5 cm**.
- The clamp spring is quite stiff, which can make installation uncomfortable.
- The cable port area is somewhat narrow and may interfere with thicker cables.

### 2. PIVA B3 Features and Performance

- Power draw is approximately **26–28 W**, close to that of the PIVA X30.
- Blue AI mode can automatically regulate power to reduce condensation.
- Suitable for leaving a phone running overnight or cooling it while charging.
- Under demanding test conditions, center temperature can drop below **17°C**.
- Includes a physical button for power and mode switching.

### 3. PIVA B3 Summary

The tradeoffs are straightforward.

#### PIVA B3 Advantages

- Reasonable price.
- Strong performance.
- Useful AI mode.
- Good build quality.

#### PIVA B3 Disadvantages

- Clamp design is not fully optimized.
- Installation and removal are somewhat difficult.

**Conclusion:** The PIVA B3 is one of the better-value choices
for users who want strong performance at a lower price.

## KIRI TH16, TH18, TH19, and TH25

According to the original content,
the **TH16, TH18, TH19, and TH25** differ mainly in appearance,
while their features and overall performance are broadly similar.

{{< figure src="https://ae-pic-a1.aliexpress-media.com/kf/S5f71ea1d9aee420780061d7b5ffc03bfN.jpg_960x960q75.jpg_.avif" alt="TH25 Image" width="960" height="960" loading="lazy" decoding="async" >}}

### 1. TH16-TH25 Overview and Accessories

- Braided USB-C to USB-C cable.
- Sturdy clamp with an integrated **1/4-inch thread** for attaching a gimbal
  or tripod.
- Included magnetic plate.

### 2. TH16-TH25 Design

- Styling inspired by the REDMAGIC Cooler 6 Pro.
- Side airflow shields reduce hot air blowing onto the user's hands.
- Well-finished USB-C port.
- Two-in-one mounting system: clamp or magnetic attachment.
- Weight of approximately **95 g**.

### 3. TH16-TH25 Features and Performance

- LED display shows power draw in watts.
- Peak power is approximately **22–23 W**.
- Includes three cooling levels, **P1, P2, and P3**, plus **AI** mode.
- Capable of rapid cooling and surface icing under some test conditions.

### 4. TH16-TH25 AI Mode and Noise

- AI mode usually regulates power around **10–11 W** to balance cooling
  performance and temperature during lighter workloads.
- Noise is acceptable overall;
  the fan becomes fairly loud only at maximum power.

### 5. TH16-TH25 Advantages and Disadvantages

The short version is below.

#### TH16-TH25 Advantages

- Attractive design.
- Good included accessories.
- Power display.
- Strong performance for the price.
- Clamp includes a 1/4-inch thread.

#### TH16-TH25 Points to Note

- The external aluminum heatsink fins can become very hot during operation.
- Compatibility may be poor with some USB power meters.

**Conclusion:** These models are worth considering in the under-250,000 VND
price range.

### TH16 V2 Compared with V1

The V2 version retains the foundation of the V1 and introduces several changes:

- **LED:** The LED design has been improved,
  giving it a more professional appearance closer to the TH12 style.

- **Features:** The AI algorithm
  and core functions are almost identical to those of the V1.

- **Limitation:** Neither version remembers its previous mode
  after power is disconnected; both return to P3 when reconnected.

## General Safety Notes

- [ ] Do not block the air intake or exhaust while the cooler is operating.
- [ ] Do not place the unit fan-side down on a table.
- [ ] Use a charger and cable with an appropriate power rating.
- [ ] Prevent condensation from flowing into the power port, circuitry, or fan.
- [ ] For metal contact surfaces,
      use PPF or another suitable protective layer to reduce the risk of
      scratching the phone's back panel.
- [ ] Do not evaluate real-world cooling effectiveness solely from no-load
      temperature measurements.
